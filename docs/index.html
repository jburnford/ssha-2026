Quinine

Ceylon flooded London with bark

By 1885 nearly all of London’s bark came from Ceylon. Then the price collapsed and the planters turned to tea.

Quinine

And quinine became cheap in Canada

Canada took nine times as much quinine in 1890 as in 1880, for about the same money.

Rubber and rice

One colony, three geographies

Tea stayed imperial. Rubber went to America, and reached Canada from there. Coconut went to the Continent.

Rubber and rice

Ghost acres of ghost acres

Rice was a quarter to a third of everything Ceylon imported. Its rubber land grew by half a million acres; its own rice land did not.
